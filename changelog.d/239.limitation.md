`bulk_create(ignore_conflicts=True)` (and `add()` / `set()` of a `ManyToManyField`) skips a row as soon as another job has inserted the same key, before that job commits; if it then rolls back, neither row is in the table. Verified with two jobs on IBM i 7.5, as are the SQL0803 / SQL0913 errors of a real race; Known Limitations now states results instead of IBM's documentation. (#239)
