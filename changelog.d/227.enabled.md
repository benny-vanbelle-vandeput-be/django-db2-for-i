- `allows_group_by_lob`: `values()`, `GROUP BY` and `DISTINCT` over a `TextField` or `BinaryField`.
- `supports_aggregate_distinct_multiple_argument` (Django 6.0+): `StringAgg(..., distinct=True)` with a separator.
- `supports_cast_with_precision`: `Cast()` to a `CharField(max_length=...)` or a `DecimalField(max_digits=..., decimal_places=...)`; a number cast to a decimal is rounded, halves away from zero, as Django expects (Db2 for i's `CAST` alone cuts off), and a float cast to text reads `3` and `4.5`, not `3E0` and `4.5E0`.
- `ignores_unnecessary_order_by_in_subqueries`: Django no longer removes an `ORDER BY` from a subquery; Db2 for i accepts it.
- `has_real_datatype`: Db2 for i has a single-precision `REAL`.
- `supports_over_clause`: window functions (`Window`), with partitions, ordering and `ROWS` / `RANGE` frames; also `supports_default_in_lead_lag` (`Lag` / `Lead` with a `default`) and `supports_frame_range_fixed_distance` (`ValueRange(start=-5, end=5)` over a number). `PercentRank` and `CumeDist` return floats.
