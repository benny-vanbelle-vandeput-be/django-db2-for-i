- **`m2m.add()`, `m2m.set()` and `bulk_create(ignore_conflicts=True)` now use `MERGE`.** For a `ManyToManyField` without a custom `through` model, Django now sends a `MERGE`, where it sent a `SELECT` and then an `INSERT` in 0.1.0b1 (with an `m2m_changed` listener it still sends the `SELECT` first). Existing rows are skipped as before. A `MERGE` can still raise an error when two jobs insert the same row at the same moment: see Known limitations below for what to catch and retry.
- **`bulk_create()` splits batches by LOB columns.** A statement takes at most 512 parameter markers for `TextField` and `BinaryField` columns, because Db2 for i refuses one with about 1,056 of them (PWS0001). That is 512 rows with one such column, 256 with two, 170 with three and 102 with five, and this applies with `ignore_conflicts` and `update_conflicts` too. The splitting is automatic, a larger `batch_size` is reduced to it, and the objects are not affected: a table with LOB columns only gets more, smaller statements. Without LOB columns the limit is 16,000 markers and 16,000 rows per statement.
