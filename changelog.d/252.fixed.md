- Four queries that Db2 for i refused (#82): `F("x") + None` (the bare `NULL` operand now gets the other operand's type), an annotation of an empty subquery (`Subquery(qs.none())`, now a typed `NULL`), an always-true condition such as `~Q(pk__in=[])` selected together with an aggregate (now `TRUE`, as Django selects it elsewhere), and a `bytes` value for a `BinaryField` (now `VARBINARY(<length>)`, or `BLOB` above 32,740 bytes; it raised `NotSupportedError`). Django's `annotations` and `expressions_case` tests are supported modules now.
- A `Value(Decimal(...))` with more digits than its `DecimalField` (`max_digits`, `decimal_places`) is sent with its own `DECIMAL(p, s)` and comes back exact, as on Django's other backends; the driver refused it ("Numeric value out of range" or "right truncation") (#95).
- `Chr` and `Ord` work for every Unicode character, also above U+FFFF (through UTF-16 surrogate pairs); before, only ASCII worked.
- Closing a cursor that is already closed does nothing, as with Django's other backends; it raised `ProgrammingError` ("Attempt to use a closed cursor").
